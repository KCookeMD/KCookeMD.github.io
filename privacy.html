Edison Health Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Edison Health is a personal, single-user application. Its only user is its owner, who uses it to access his own health data.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission through Google's sign-in, Edison Health reads data from the Google Health API: activity and fitness, health metrics and measurements, sleep, and nutrition. It can also write activity, health metric and sleep entries that the owner creates.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show the owner summaries of his own health and to answer his questions about it. It is not used for advertising, profiling of other people, or any purpose other than the owner's personal use.

Where the data is stored

Data is processed and stored on the owner's own computer. Sign-in credentials are stored locally in a file readable only by the owner's account.

Sharing

Edison Health does not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone. It does not transfer data to third parties, except to the extent the owner himself chooses to use the data with his own tools.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Edison Health's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections. Locally stored data and credentials can be deleted by the owner at any time.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email shown on Edison Health's Google sign-in screen.
