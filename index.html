Edison Health

A personal health dashboard for one user.

Edison Health is a private, single-user tool. Its owner uses it to read his own health data (activity, sleep, heart rate, body measurements and nutrition) from the Google Health API, so that his personal assistant software can summarize trends and answer questions about his own health.

It is not a public service. It has no sign-up, serves no other users, and is not offered for sale. Access is limited to the owner's own Google account.
